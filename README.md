# what is the nexus onion mirror market


the nexus onion mirror market is a version of the original nexus darknet market. it runs on the tor network and uses an .onion address. people use it to access the market when the main link is down or blocked.


# why mirrors matter


darknet markets often go offline. sometimes it's due to law enforcement. other times, it's technical issues. mirror sites help users stay connected. they offer the same content as the main site but through a different url.


# how to find a working nexus mirror


you won’t find official nexus onion mirror links on regular search engines. they’re shared through trusted forums, vendor messages, or verified community posts. always check the pgp signature if one is provided. this helps confirm the link is real and not a scam.


# stay safe when using mirrors


not every mirror is safe. some are run by scammers to steal your login or funds. never enter your credentials on a site you’re unsure about. use tor browser. disable scripts when possible. and never reuse passwords.


# is nexus still active


as of now, nexus has had periods of downtime. but when it’s up, it’s known for strict vendor rules and strong security. that’s why users look for working mirrors - to get back in quickly and safely.
